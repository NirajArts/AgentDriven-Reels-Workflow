# Visual and motion choices

The brief determines palette, type, imagery, layout and motion style. Use professional typography, recordings, animation, diagrams or generated assets as appropriate to the subject; no look is preselected by this package.

Plan headings as visual elements: entrance, emphasis, readable hold and exit. Animate where it supports the chosen style and attention. Preserve sharp settled text, deliberate hierarchy and legibility at the intended viewing size. Keep critical controls and captions clear.

Use acceleration, purposeful travel and a controlled settle rather than mechanical linear movement everywhere. For a deliberately snappy style, a useful starting experiment is zero endpoint speeds and roughly 80-90% temporal influence over 12-24 frames at 30 fps; tune distance and timing to the brief. These are starting values, not a universal preset. Continuous playheads and sampled physical motion may require linear interpolation between samples to avoid micro-stops. Inspect evaluated motion, not only easing labels.

If a software demonstration uses a cursor, target a meaningful control, stop, act and show the result. Avoid idle wiggle, clicks before arrival and overlays covering the target. Protect readable holds and preserve honest causality. Use motion blur selectively and inspect extreme poses/transitions.
